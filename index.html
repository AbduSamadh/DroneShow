How it works

Before every move, each drone is matched to a spot so that no two straight-line paths come close, and all drones leave and arrive together. Switch flight paths to Random to see what happens without that planning.

Turn on Paths and Slow together to watch the routes while they fly. Export saves every formation as per-drone waypoints.
